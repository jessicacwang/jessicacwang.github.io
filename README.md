# Hello, my name is Jessica C. Wang

and this is my personal website.

## Professionally,

I am finishing up my M.S. in computational linguistics at the University of Washington, under the advisement by [Amandalynne Paullada](https://www.amandalynne.pw/index.html) and [Shane Steinert-Threlkeld](https://www.shane.st/). My master's thesis seeks to describe how different English regional dialects and genres are affected by the notion of "text quality" for LLM pretraining. 



### My interests include (but are not limited to)
- how sociolinguistic theories can inform the development of language technology,
- globalization and localization (broadly construed), 
- data attribution, and
- structured document summarization
